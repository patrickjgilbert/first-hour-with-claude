*Practice file. Northwind is a fictional company; every person and address here is made up.*

## How I like answers
- Lead with the answer, then the detail.
- Anything that needs a decision from me goes at the top.
- Calculate numbers from the files; don't estimate. Say where a number came from.
- If the files don't say, tell me. Don't fill the gap.

## When I ask you to "check in with" someone
Draft the message in the channel they prefer (above), in my voice (see VOICE.md), about the live item they own. Keep it to a few lines. Show me the draft; don't send anything.
