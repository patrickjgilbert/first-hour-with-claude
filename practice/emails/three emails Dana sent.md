# Three emails Dana sent

*Practice file. Dana Whitfield owns Northwind Home Services, a fictional company. These are three real emails from her sent folder, used to learn how she writes.*

---

**To:** Mr. and Mrs. Pelletier
**Subject:** Tuesday's visit

Hi Anne and Marc,

I heard from Sofia that we got to you late on Tuesday and that nobody called ahead. That is on us, and I'm sorry. The window is the whole point and we missed it.

I've taken an hour off the invoice for that visit. You'll see it as a $45 credit. You don't need to do anything.

I'd also like to make sure it doesn't happen again. Would an earlier window work better for you going forward? Our 8 to 10 slot is almost never late. If that's easier for your week, just reply and I'll have Priya move you.

Thanks so much for sticking with us. It means a lot.

Dana

---

**To:** Everyone at Northwind
**Subject:** Three new faces on October 5

Hi all,

We've got three new cleaners starting Monday, October 5. Tom will introduce them at the morning huddle. Their names are on the whiteboard.

A few things I'm asking from everyone that first week:

- If you see them standing around, give them something to do and show them how. Nobody learns our way by watching.
- Crew leads, please do the walkthrough with them at the first job of the day, every day that week. Even if it feels slow.
- If you notice something we should have told them and didn't, write it down and give it to Tom. We're building a real checklist this time.

This is a busy stretch and I know adding people slows you down before it speeds you up. Thank you for being patient with it.

One ask: by Friday, send Tom one sentence about how the new folks are doing. Good or bad, I want to hear it.

Thanks so much!

Dana

---

**To:** Pete at Casco Auto and Truck
**Subject:** Van #2 quote

Hi Pete,

Thanks for turning the quote around so fast. $3,400 for the transmission is about what I expected, which is to say more than I wanted.

Before I say yes, two questions:

1. Is there anything else on that van that's close to going? It's nine years old and I don't want to put $3,400 into it and then have the brakes go in December.
2. If we did the work, how long would you need it? We're short a van as it is.

If you think the honest answer is "don't fix it," tell me that. I'd rather hear it from you than find out the hard way.

Can you get back to me by Wednesday? I need to decide before the new hires start.

Thanks so much,

Dana
