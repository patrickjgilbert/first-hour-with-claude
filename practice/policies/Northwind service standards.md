# Northwind Home Services: service standards

*Practice file. Northwind Home Services is a fictional company. This is the version of the policy we hand to new hires and send to customers who ask.*

Northwind is a residential cleaning and handyman company in Portland, Maine. We run two cleaning crews (Crew A and Crew B) and one handyman crew (Crew H). About 300 customers trust us with their homes and buildings. These standards are how we keep that trust. They apply to every job, every crew, every day.

## Arrival windows

Every job is booked into a two-hour arrival window, for example 8:00 to 10:00. The customer sees the window when they book and again in the reminder text the night before.

We do not promise an exact arrival time. Traffic on the bridge, a job that runs long, a flat tire: these happen. The window gives us room to be reliable without being rigid.

If a crew knows it will arrive after the window closes, the crew lead texts or calls the customer before the window ends, not after. A heads-up at 9:45 for a 10:00 window is on time. A heads-up at 10:20 is an apology.

## What counts as on time

A job is on time if the crew is at the door at or before the end of the window. Arriving at 10:00 for an 8:00 to 10:00 window counts. Arriving at 10:01 does not.

Arriving early is fine if the customer has agreed to it. Do not knock before the window opens without a text first. Many customers plan their morning around the window.

We track on-time arrival for every job in the jobs file. The crew lead records the actual arrival time when the crew walks in, not when the van parks. Rounding in our favor is not allowed.

## The credit policy

If we miss the window, the customer gets one free hour of labor. We apply it as a $45 credit on that visit's invoice, or on the next visit if the invoice already went out. The customer does not have to ask. The crew lead tells them at the door and notes it on the job sheet.

If we miss the window twice in a row for the same customer, the office manager calls them that day and Dana follows up personally within the week.

Commercial accounts with a monthly invoice get the same credit per missed window, totaled on their monthly statement.

## Cancellations and rescheduling

Customers can cancel or move a job for free up to 24 hours before the window opens. Inside 24 hours we charge a $60 late-cancellation fee, which we waive for illness, weather, or a family emergency. We do not argue with customers about which category they fall into.

If we cancel on a customer inside 24 hours, their next visit of the same type is half price.

A lockout (the crew arrives and cannot get in, and the customer cannot be reached within 20 minutes) is billed as a late cancellation.

## What crews never do

These are hard rules. No exceptions, no matter who asks or how nicely.

- We do not move furniture over 50 pounds. We clean around it or under it as far as we can reach. If a customer wants a heavy piece moved, they move it before we arrive or we schedule a handyman visit with two people and the right equipment.
- We do not climb above the second rung of a ladder outside. Window washing above that height is referred to a specialty vendor. Inside, the step stool and the two-step ladder are the limit for cleaning crews.
- We do not handle valuables. Jewelry, cash, watches, documents, medication, firearms. If something like this is in the way, we leave it where it is and clean around it. If we find something that looks lost, we leave it in place and tell the customer.
- We do not use the customer's chemicals unless the job sheet says so. Mixing products is dangerous and we cannot vouch for what is in an unlabeled bottle.
- We do not let anyone into the home who is not on the job sheet. That includes a neighbor who says the customer told them it was fine.
- We do not take photos inside a customer's home except for the before-and-after shots the customer has agreed to, and those stay in the job app.

## The review follow-up rule

Every review, good or bad, gets a reply from the office within two business days.

A review of 3 stars or lower gets a phone call, not just a reply. The customer care lead makes the call, finds out what happened, and logs it in the customer's notes. If the problem was ours, the customer gets the one-hour credit whether or not the window was missed.

Three or more low reviews about the same crew in a month triggers a sit-down between the operations lead and that crew lead. This is a conversation, not a write-up. Most of the time the cause is a scheduling problem, not a people problem.

We never ask a customer to change or remove a review. We never offer anything in exchange for one.

## Keys, codes and pets

Keys are tagged with the customer ID only, never the address. They live in the lockbox in the office and go out with the crew lead in the morning and come back at night. Alarm and gate codes are stored in the customer file, not written on the key tag.

Pets stay wherever the customer has put them. Do not let a dog out into the yard "just for a minute" unless the job sheet says it is fine.

## Questions

If a situation is not covered here, the rule is simple: do what you would want done in your own home, and call the office before you do anything you are unsure about. The office would always rather get the call.
